📖 How To Use

Step by Step Guide

EdmFire use karna bahut aasaan hai — neeche diye gaye steps follow karo aur apna pehla tournament join karo!

01
Create an Account
EdmFire app kholo aur Gmail account + password se sign up karo.
02
Login to the App
Registration ke baad same email aur password se login karo.
03
Verify Free Fire UID (KYC)
Profile section mein jao → KYC button click karo → apna real Free Fire Max UID enter karo → India select karo → verify karo.
04
Add Coins to Wallet
UPI apps jaise PhonePe ya Paytm se virtual coins kharido. Payment ke baad UTR/Bank Ref. No. app mein verify karo.
05
Join a Tournament
Available tournaments browse karo — Solo, Duo ya Squad — wallet coins se entry do.
06
Receive Room Details
Match shuru hone se pehle Room ID aur Password app mein display hoga.
07
Play the Match
Free Fire Max mein room join karo aur tournament mein participate karo.
08
Win Rewards
Winning players ke rewards in-app wallet mein credit kiye jaate hain.
09
Withdraw Rewards
Wallet balance ko UPI ya Redeem Code ke through withdraw kar sakte ho.

💡 Tip: KYC complete karne ke baad hi tournament join kar sakte ho — pehle KYC zaroor karo!
