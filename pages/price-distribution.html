How Rewards Are Distributed

Tournament rewards are distributed according to the specific prize structure of each tournament hosted on EdmFire.

Step 1
🏆 Reward Credit
After tournament results are verified, winning rewards are credited directly to the player's in-app wallet.
Step 2
👛 Wallet Balance
Players can view their reward balance anytime in the wallet section of the app.
Step 3
📤 Withdrawal Request
Players may request withdrawal of wallet balance through the available withdrawal system in the app.
Step 4
💸 Payment Processing
Approved withdrawals are processed using supported payout methods such as UPI (PhonePe, Paytm, etc.).
Step 5
✅ Verification Process
Prize distribution may require result verification to ensure fair play and accurate tournament outcomes.

Platform Rights

EDMFire Technologies reserves the right to verify match results before releasing rewards. EdmFire maintains a fair and transparent reward distribution system for all players.
