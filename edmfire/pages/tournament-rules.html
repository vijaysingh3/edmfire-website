Tournament Rules

Ye rules EdmFire platform pe host kiye gaye sabhi tournaments pe apply hote hain. Sabhi players in rules ko follow karna zaroori hai.

Rule 01
Eligibility
Players ke paas verified Free Fire UID aur valid EdmFire account hona zaroori hai. Bina KYC ke tournament join nahi kar sakte.
Rule 02
Tournament Types
Solo, Duo aur Squad — teeno formats available hain. Har tournament ke specific rules alag ho sakte hain.
Rule 03
Entry Requirements
Players ko virtual coins se entry fee pay karni hogi aur match time pe room join karna hoga.
Rule 04
Fair Play
Cheating, hacking, ya bugs exploit karna strictly prohibited hai. Violation pe instant permanent ban aur prize forfeiture hoga.
Rule 05
Emulator Restriction
Emulators are NOT allowed. Emulator players will be instantly disqualified from the tournament.
Rule 06
VPN Usage
VPN may be allowed unless it provides unfair gameplay advantage. Suspicious VPN usage may lead to disqualification.
Rule 07
Prize Distribution
Tournament rewards are credited to in-app wallet after result verification. Withdrawal via UPI. All withdrawals require verification.
Rule 08
Disqualification
Players may be disqualified for: cheating, late joining, incorrect UID, or any rule violation.

⚠️ EdmFire is not affiliated with Garena Free Fire and is intended for entertainment purposes only.
