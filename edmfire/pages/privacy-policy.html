1. Introduction

Welcome to EdmFire, operated by EDMFire Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

EdmFire is an online gaming tournament platform designed for entertainment purposes where users participate in tournaments using virtual coins within the application.

⚠️ By accessing or using the EdmFire application, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information from users to operate the application securely and effectively.

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Username or nickname
  • Password (handled securely via Firebase Authentication)

Game Verification Information

Users verify their Free Fire Max Game UID inside the app to participate in tournaments.

Device Information

  • Android Device ID (for security and fraud prevention)

Notification Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for sending notifications

Analytics Data

  • Anonymous usage statistics via Firebase services

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Government identification documents
  • Bank account details
  • Credit card information

4. How We Use Information

  • Account creation and management
  • Game UID verification
  • Prevent fraud and multiple accounts
  • Manage tournaments
  • Send notifications
  • Improve app performance

5. Wallet and Virtual Coins

EdmFire uses a virtual coin system. Users may purchase coins using UPI methods such as PhonePe, Paytm or other UPI-enabled apps.

⚠️ EdmFire is not a real-money gambling application. The platform is designed for entertainment purposes only.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google Play Services

7. Push Notifications

Users may receive notifications for tournament reminders, results, promotions, and updates.

8. Age Requirement

Users must be at least 13 years old to use the EdmFire platform.

9. Multiple Account Policy

Only one account per device and per user is allowed.
